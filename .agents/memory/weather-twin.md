---
name: Weather Twin guidance
description: Product rule for turning shared forecast signals into role-specific decisions.
---

Recommendations should adapt to the user's role while showing the weather signals that produced the advice. The app's guidance is planning support, not an official emergency or marine warning.

**Why:** The product direction is to move from weather information to weather → risk → personalized action without hiding the reasoning or overstating certainty.

**How to apply:** Add new profiles or action flows by reusing live forecast inputs, exposing the relevant drivers, and linking severe, flood, or marine situations to official IMD and local authority sources.
